## Basic Gear

During the early game, **don't worry too much about your basic gear**. Simply keep upgrading it with better drops as you progress.

Basic gear has several mechanics that allow you to improve its stats:

### ✨ Enchantment

A subsystem that uses **Enhance Stones**, which can be obtained through mining, to enchant your equipment and increase its stats.

There are **many different enchantment tiers**, so keep upgrading your gear whenever you have enough materials.

### 🔥 Fusion

A system that allows you to increase your equipment's stats by **up to 50%**.

Fusion requires **up to 3 copies of the same equipment**.

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/zjuve/Crazy-Odyssey/blob/main/Crazy%20Odyssey%20-%20PNGS/fusion.png" width="700">
</p>

### 💀 Soul Infusion

A subsystem that **greatly increases your equipment's stats**.

It uses equipment of the **same or lower tier** to increase the stats of your main piece. It also provides **reduced drop chance during PvP**.

There are **12 Soul Infusion tiers**.

---

### 💡 General Tips

These systems are **useful for every type of player**, whether you're F2P or P2W.

* Upgrade your gear whenever possible.
* **Trade gear with your guild members** instead of letting useful pieces sit unused.
* Make good use of your **Storage** to keep equipment for future upgrades or trades.
* Don't waste too many resources trying to perfect early-game gear. **You'll replace it as you progress.**

> **TL;DR:** Keep upgrading your basic gear, use Fusion and Soul Infusion when available, and don't overinvest in equipment that you'll replace later.
