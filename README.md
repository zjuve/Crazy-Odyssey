# Introduction to the Game

First of all, we need to accept that **Crazy Odyssey is a P2W game**. Because of this, I will try to divide the guides into three categories based on how much money a player is willing to spend.

### 1. F2P

These guides will focus on players who are **completely free-to-play**, meaning they will not spend a single dollar on the game.

### 2. Low–Mid Spender

These guides will focus on players who spend money on the game, with a monthly budget of **up to $150 USD**.

### 3. High Spender & Whales

There will probably not be many guides specifically aimed at these players, since **I am a low-to-mid spender myself**.

## What Is Crazy Odyssey?

**Crazy Odyssey** on Android, and **Crazy Oddballs** on Steam, is a **Chinese Chuanqi MMORPG** developed by Chinese developers.

It is a true MMORPG, and **playing and interacting with other players is very important**, for reasons that will become clear throughout this guide.

Unlike many other so-called "MMORPGs" on Android, **player trading is actually relevant in this game**. Many useful items can be traded between players, which allows F2P players to make meaningful progress without spending money.

### What Is Chuanqi?

**Chuanqi (传奇)** means **"Legend"** and refers to a niche subgenre of Chinese MMORPGs.

These games are typically characterized by:

* 💥 Explosive loot drops
* ⚔️ Heavy PvP
* 🏰 Guilds and alliances
* 👥 Strong player interaction
* 📈 Large amounts of character progression

Crazy Odyssey has **many different mechanics and progression systems** designed to improve your character's performance across all types of content, both **PvP and PvE**.

---

## ⚠️ Karma & Looting System

It is important to understand that **you can drop loot when you die if you kill too many players and your name becomes red**.

Because of this, **remember to clear your karma regularly**.

> **IMPORTANT:** Always store your valuable items in your **Storage** so you don't lose them if you forget to clear your karma.

It is also recommended to **carry some junk items** so that, in the worst-case scenario, those items are more likely to be dropped instead of your valuable equipment.

The equipment you are currently wearing has a **lower chance of being dropped**, and there are also mechanics in the game that further reduce the chance of losing equipped items.

# 1. Equipment

### A. Basic Gear

Basic Gear consists of **10 equipment slots**, each of which provides different stats.

### B. Special Rings

There are several types of **Special Rings** available in the game, each providing different bonuses and effects.

### C. Divine Gear

**Divine Gear** is extremely rare and powerful equipment that provides large amounts of stats as well as **special abilities**.

### D. Dragon Gear

**Dragon Gear** is powerful special equipment that is **linked to Basic Gear**, providing additional stats and abilities.

---

# 2. Secondary Systems

### A. Divine Seals

There are **6 Divine Seals**, which provide essential stats for PvP.

Each seal has:

* **15 tiers**
* **5 enhancement stages**

### B. Wings

Wings are an essential part of character progression because they provide several highly relevant stats.

#### I. Wingsuit

Wingsuit is a **tertiary wing equipment system** that provides large amounts of stats.

It has:

* **15 tiers**
* **5 enhancement stages**

#### II. Divine Wing

Divine Wings are **rare and powerful tertiary wing equipment** that provide additional stats and special abilities.

### C. Four Symbols

The **Four Symbols** provide important stats, particularly for PvP.

#### I. Sub Gear

Each symbol has **6 Sub Gear slots**.

These pieces have their own **tiers and enhancement levels**.

#### II. Divine Gear

Each symbol also has **6 Divine Gear slots**.

These are rare and powerful pieces that provide **important PvP stats and special abilities**.

---

# 3. Divine Might

**Divine Might** is an achievement-based progression system that provides various stats and a title.

It contains multiple objectives that must be completed in order to increase your score and reach higher tiers.

---

# 4. Bestiary

Each relevant monster has a chance to drop a **Bestiary Card**.

These cards can provide:

* Boss-related bonuses
* PvP bonuses
* Additional stats

Later on, these cards can be **upgraded to increase their stats and effectiveness**.

---

# 5. Deitification

**Deitification** is a progression system based on completing various objectives.

Completing these objectives provides:

* Various character stats
* Talent Points

Talent Points can then be used to increase your **effectiveness and resistance against the three classes in the game**.

---

# 6. Mounts

Mounts are a progression system that includes **equipment and stats**.

You need to use **Mount EXP** to level up your mount, and you can also **reroll the stats** provided by the mount.

---

# 7. Pets

There are many different types of **Pets**, and they are all important for character progression.

Choosing and improving the right pets is an important part of becoming stronger.

---

# 8. Skills

The **Skill system** is complex and includes many different variations and subsystems.

Understanding how these systems interact is essential for optimizing your character.

---

# 9. Soul Rings

**Soul Rings** are one of the most important progression systems in Crazy Odyssey.

They allow players to **customize and specialize their builds**, making them a key part of both PvE and PvP progression.
